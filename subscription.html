Common questions

Quick answers about billing and plans.
How do I pay?

Online payment is not available yet. Send a plan request and the BusinessOS team will share payment details. Your plan is activated after the payment is verified.

What happens when I reach my limit?

On the Free plan, adding new customers or leads stops at 10 records. Upgrade whenever you're ready — your existing customers and data stay exactly as they are.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. Send a new request for the plan you want, or contact us for a custom Business plan with higher limits.

Paid access is activated manually by the BusinessOS team after payment verification.
