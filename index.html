Questions owners ask first.

Is my customer data safe?

Your data is stored in Google Firebase, and only people who sign in to your workspace can open it.

Does WhatsApp send messages automatically?

No. BusinessOS opens the chat with your message already written. You review it and press send.

Can I try it before I sign up?

Yes. The live demo lets you click through a sample workspace with no sign-up.

Can my team use it?

Yes. Starter includes 3 team members and Growth includes 10.

What happens when I reach the free limit?

The free plan holds 10 combined customer and lead records. To add more, move to a paid plan.
