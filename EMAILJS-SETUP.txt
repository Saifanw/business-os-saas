BUSINESSOS DIRECT EMAIL SETUP (EmailJS)

1. Create an EmailJS account and connect the sender email service.
2. Create an EmailJS email template with these fields:
   - To Email: {{to_email}}
   - Subject: {{subject}}
   - Message/body: {{message}}
   - Optional greeting/name: {{to_name}}
   - Optional sender/business label: {{business_name}}
   - Optional Reply-To: {{reply_to}}
3. Copy the Service ID, Template ID, and Public Key into email-config.js.
4. Deploy the three files together: customers.html, customer-list.html, email-config.js.
5. Test using a customer record with an email address you control before using real customer addresses.

Important:
- EmailJS credentials are placeholders in email-config.js until you add your own IDs.
- The browser-side Public Key is intended for client use; do not add private server/API secrets.
- Configure allowed domains/rate limits in EmailJS to reduce abuse.
- Activity is recorded as Email sent only after EmailJS reports success. Delivery to the recipient inbox can still depend on the email provider.
